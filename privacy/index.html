Privacy notice.

About the information you choose to share through this website.

Project enquiries

When you send the contact form, you provide your name, email address, selected area of interest, message and, if you choose, organisation. Neustar uses this information to understand and respond to your enquiry and discuss relevant work with you.

Contact form processing

The form sends your enquiry to Neustar through Formspree, the contact-form service used by this website. Formspree processes the submission as part of delivering the message. You can read its privacy policy for information about its processing.

Video, fonts and browsing

The website’s video, images and fonts are served from the site itself. The silent background video starts automatically where browser settings allow it, without a visible playback control. Devices that request reduced motion, use data-saving settings or report a 2G connection receive a still image instead. Playback pauses when the video leaves view or the browser tab is hidden. This version of the website does not add advertising trackers, analytics cookies or newsletter subscriptions.

Sharing and external links

Article share buttons open the selected external service or your device’s share control. External websites have their own privacy practices. A copied article link is written to your clipboard only when you choose the share action and your browser supports it.

Contact about your information

For questions about an enquiry, or to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent, email business@neustar.com.ng.
