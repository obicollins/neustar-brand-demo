Neustar is a technology company based in Lagos, Nigeria. We bring software, artificial intelligence, infrastructure, hardware and design into the same conversation because real problems cross the boundaries between them.

Our philosophy begins with a simple conviction from our original story: effective systems underpin efficient enterprises. We want to understand what a system is for, who it serves and how it fits the world around it.

That asks us to be philosophers before we choose a direction, coders when an idea needs logic, and builders when it is time to make the parts work together. Curiosity opens the question. Judgment gives it shape. Craft makes it real.

Our core values

The principles beneath
the practice.

Service, research, talent, innovation and insight remain the foundations of Neustar’s philosophy.

Service

Begin with the people the technology serves. Make their work, decisions and experiences better.

Research

Ask good questions, examine assumptions and use evidence to decide what should be built.

Talent

Bring thoughtful people and complementary disciplines together around the problem.

Innovation

Explore boldly, then apply the judgment and craft that turn a possibility into something useful.

Insight

Connect information with context. Make the next decision clearer, for ourselves and for our clients.

Our approach

Consider the whole.
Care for the parts.

A good process creates clarity as it creates the product. We make the reasoning visible and keep the work grounded in the outcome.

Understand the real question

Listen to the people doing the work. Understand the context, constraints and intended outcome before choosing the tools.

Shape the whole system

Make the architecture, information and interfaces coherent. Explain the choices, the dependencies and the trade-offs.

Make the idea tangible

Bring design and engineering together. Use a focused prototype or release to examine the assumptions that matter.

Build for what comes next

Plan for operation, documentation, handover and change. Useful technology has a life beyond the first launch.
