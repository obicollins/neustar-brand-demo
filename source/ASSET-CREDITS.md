The source clip was shortened to eight seconds, scaled to 1280×720, encoded as H.264 without audio and optimised for streaming. The delivered MP4 is 700,222 bytes. The still fallback is a 30,076-byte WebP frame from that clip. Original downloads and temporary encoding tools are excluded from the deliverable.

## Original website illustrations
The N sculpture, AI workflow model, software architecture model and display-space illustration were generated specifically for the Neustar rebuild using OpenAI's image-generation tool, then encoded as WebP for delivery. They are conceptual editorial images and are not client-project photography. The actual brand mark and icons are code-native SVG artwork.

## Logo concepts

The three full-name identity concepts in `logo-concepts/` were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 20 October 2026. The owner clarified that the selected identity is **Orbital star, the logo with the star**, resolving the earlier option-number mix-up. Its original raster presentation remains `1-orbital-star.png`. The folded-star direction is implemented as code-native SVG production artwork in graphite/slate (`#282F30`), sage (`#D6EC9C`) and ivory (`#EEEEE7`), including reversed versions for dark backgrounds. All three original raster presentations and generation prompts are retained in `logo-concepts/` and `logo-concepts/PROMPTS.md`; these large review images are not loaded by the website.
